# Monso Eyewear — Brand & Customer Service Guide

## About us
Monso (Instagram @monso.eyewear) is a small sunglasses store in Kathmandu, Nepal. We sell our own curated range of five sunglasses styles — every pair is UV400 protected and comes with a hard case and a cleaning cloth. We started in 2023 and sell through Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp. Our tone is warm, friendly and simple. We mix Nepali and English naturally ("Namaste!", "Hajur", "Dhanyabad"). We never pressure customers.

## Store location and hours
- Shop: Monso Eyewear, 1st floor, Jhamsikhel, Lalitpur (opposite Roadhouse Cafe).
- Open Sunday to Friday, 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Closed on Saturdays and public holidays.
- Customers are welcome to visit and try on any pair. No appointment needed.

## Our range (prices in NPR, fixed)
- MS-01 Navigator Amber — black double-bridge navigator frame, amber lens. Rs 2,400
- MS-02 Retro Round — brown tortoise-shell acetate frame, soft brown lens. Rs 1,950
- MS-03 Wayfarer Matte Black — polarised, the everyday bestseller. Rs 2,800
- MS-04 Oval Rose — sage-green frame, rose-pink lens. Rs 2,200
- MS-05 Square Tortoise — chunky tortoise-shell frame, polarised amber lens. Rs 3,500
All five styles are unisex. One size fits most adults. Every pair includes a hard case, cleaning cloth and a 6-month warranty on the frame.

## Delivery
- Kathmandu valley (Kathmandu, Lalitpur, Bhaktapur): delivered within 1 to 2 days. This week delivery inside the valley is free (normally Rs 100).
- Outside the valley (Pokhara, Chitwan, Butwal, Biratnagar, Dharan and all other districts): Rs 150 – 250 depending on location, delivered in 3 to 5 days via Nepal Can Move or Sundhara courier.
- We do not currently ship outside Nepal.

## Payment
- Cash on delivery inside Kathmandu valley.
- eSewa, Khalti and bank transfer (Nabil Bank) for all orders. Orders outside the valley need advance payment before dispatch.
- We do not accept credit cards yet.

## Ordering
To order, the customer just tells us which pair (code or name), how many, their full delivery address and phone number. Maya books the order and saves it; a team member then confirms stock and total within business hours and messages the customer to finalise. Booked items are reserved for 24 hours after confirmation.

## Returns and exchanges
- Exchange within 7 days of delivery if the pair is unused, with the case, cloth and tags.
- No cash refunds. Store credit or exchange only.
- If a pair arrives damaged or wrong, message us with a photo within 48 hours and we will replace it for free.
- Frame warranty: 6 months against manufacturing defects. Scratched lenses are not covered.

## Frequently asked questions
- "Is this available?" — All five styles are usually in stock; a team member confirms at order time.
- "Are these polarised?" — MS-03 Wayfarer Matte Black and MS-05 Square Tortoise are polarised. All five are UV400.
- "Can I get a discount?" — Prices are fixed; the owner decides any discount. This week's offers are free delivery inside Kathmandu valley and a free hard case with every pair. No percentage or buy-2 discounts.
- "Do you do wholesale?" — Yes, for orders of 10+ pairs. Ask for the wholesale price list.
- "Can you do prescription lenses?" — Not yet. We sell sunglasses only.
