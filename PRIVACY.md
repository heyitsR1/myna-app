# Privacy Policy — Myna

**Last updated:** 25 August 2026

Myna ("we", "us") provides a unified messaging inbox that helps small businesses read and reply to customer messages from Instagram, WhatsApp and Facebook in one place, with optional AI-drafted replies. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what choices you have.

If you have questions, contact us at **toolsmxpo@gmail.com**.

## Who this policy covers

- **Business users** — the shop or business owner who connects their social accounts to Myna.
- **Customers** — people who send messages to a business that uses Myna.

## What we collect

**From connected messaging accounts.** When a business connects an Instagram, WhatsApp or Facebook account, we receive:

- Messages sent to that business account, including message text and timestamps
- The sender's platform-assigned user ID, and their username or display name where the platform provides it
- The business account's ID and username
- Access tokens issued by the platform, used only to read and send messages on the business's behalf

**From the business user.** Business documents uploaded to train AI replies — price lists, delivery policies, FAQs and similar. Whatever the business chooses to upload.

**Automatically.** Basic technical logs such as error records and request timestamps, used to keep the service running.

We do **not** collect payment card details, government ID numbers, or precise location data.

## How we use it

- To display incoming messages to the business in one inbox
- To generate suggested replies using the business's own uploaded documents as context
- To send replies back to the customer through the original platform, when the business approves them or has enabled automatic replies
- To maintain, debug and improve the service

We do **not** sell personal data, and we do **not** use message content to train AI models offered to other businesses.

## How AI replies work

Suggested replies are generated by a language model running on infrastructure controlled by the business or by us — not by a third-party AI provider. Message content is not sent to external AI services.

The AI drafts a reply using the business's uploaded documents. When the model is not confident, or the message involves money, complaints, or anything outside those documents, the draft is held for a human to review before sending.

## Who we share it with

We share personal data only with:

- **The messaging platforms themselves** (Meta), to receive and send messages — this is inherent to the service
- **Our hosting and database provider**, which stores data on our behalf under contract
- **Authorities**, where we are legally required to do so

Every business's data is kept separate. One business cannot see another's messages or documents.

## Where data is stored

Message and document data is stored on managed database infrastructure in the Asia-Pacific (Singapore) region.

## How long we keep it

- **Messages** — retained while the business's account is active, so conversation history stays available
- **Uploaded documents** — until the business deletes them
- **Access tokens** — until the business disconnects the account or the token expires

When a business closes its Myna account, we delete its messages, documents and tokens within 30 days.

## Your choices and rights

Businesses can, at any time:

- Disconnect a messaging account, which stops all further data collection from it
- Delete uploaded documents
- Turn off automatic AI replies, so nothing is sent without human approval
- Request a copy of their data, or request deletion, by emailing us

Customers who message a business using Myna can ask that business to delete their conversation, or contact us directly at the address above. We will act on verified requests within 30 days.

## Data deletion requests

To request deletion of your data, email **toolsmxpo@gmail.com** with the subject line "Data deletion request" and the Instagram, WhatsApp or Facebook username involved. We will confirm once the data is removed.

## Children

Myna is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Security

Access tokens and message data are stored with access restricted at the database level so each business can reach only its own records. Tokens are never exposed to browsers. Transport is encrypted in transit.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we will update the date above and notify connected businesses.
